About Bees

Bees are remarkable flying insects vital to our planet's survival, functioning as nature's primary pollinators. By transferring pollen between flowering plants, they facilitate the growth of numerous fruits, vegetables, and seeds that make up a significant portion of the human diet. These social creatures typically live in highly organized colonies led by a single queen, supported by drones and industrious workers. Beyond their ecological role, honeybees are famous for producing honey and wax, resources humans have utilized for millennia. However, bee populations currently face global threats from habitat loss and pesticides, making their conservation essential for maintaining biodiversity and food security.
